## 2024 Season

**Lead**: [LOGIC-XYZ (MiZoReCanvas)](https://github.com/LOGIC-XYZ)

### Defense record

- INS accuracy and error measurement (roll, pitch, velocity, and position accuracy)
- Cost trade-offs (LiDAR, INS, camera)
- Multi-sensor fusion
- Why choose EKF instead of alternatives such as UKF
- What happens when the camera fails (cone reflective strips missing), the LiDAR fails, or the INS fails
- How to handle a failure in a middle frame
- Differences between simulation and reality (steering angle, coordinates)
- Differences between mapping and reality

## 2025 Season

**Lead**: [LOGIC-XYZ (MiZoReCanvas)](https://github.com/LOGIC-XYZ)

### Defense record

- Mapping accuracy: cone accuracy and vehicle pose accuracy — how to evaluate them, and with what data
- Loop-closure detection approach
- Failure of any single sensor or of multiple sensors (LiDAR, camera, integrated INS)

<Aside type="note">
The rules actually require entering EBS when sensors fail, but a corresponding scheme can still be designed.
</Aside>

- Equipment cost trade-offs (LiDAR, INS, camera)
- For the 2025 season roll-up banner, the judges noted that the text descriptions were a bit sparse

## Season exchanges

### South China University of Technology

Last year, their defense also asked about multi-sensor fusion and sensor failure in extreme environments.

This year they tried a constraint approach similar to factor-graph optimization, which demands a lot of compute. They suggested starting local optimization before all cones are scanned to reduce the compute requirement.

### Tongji University

This year, their defense asked about relocalization.

Their mapping approach is registration-based mapping: matching against their map.

## Optimization ideas

<CardGrid>
  <Card title="Fix first-frame cone coordinates" icon="approve-check">
    Fix the cone coordinates detected in the first frame and never update them afterwards. The figure-eight track only needs those six cones, and the earliest detections are the most accurate.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Trajectory visualization" icon="seti:image">
    Visualize the vehicle's driving trajectory.
  </Card>
  <Card title="State equation optimization" icon="setting">
    Try using Kalman filters to set up three state equations for the three track segments — straight-line acceleration, high-speed tracking, and the figure-eight skidpad — with nonlinearity increasing gradually.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Time synchronization" icon="rocket">
    The defense mentioned that time-synchronized point-cloud distortion compensation showed little noticeable effect. The judges said the front-end filtering may simply be too good, but it is still needed. On the hard-sync side, wire RS-232 & PPS to the LiDAR.
  </Card>
</CardGrid>

<Aside type="tip">
Or try factor-graph optimization — there are no concrete ideas yet, so feel free to keep exploring.
</Aside>
